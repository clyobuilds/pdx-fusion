About PDX Fusion

PDX Fusion is a community calendar for Portland's fusion dance scene. Here you can find both recurring and one-off dance socials, lessons, workshops, and festivals, all in one place. It is maintained by members of the community and not affiliated with any specific event or organization.

Adding an event

Anyone can submit an event with the Add Event button. New events are reviewed before they appear, usually quickly. Event organizers can approve and edit all event submissions.

Stay up-to-date

You can subscribe to the calendar in your own calendar app or feed reader using the Share button on the home page.
